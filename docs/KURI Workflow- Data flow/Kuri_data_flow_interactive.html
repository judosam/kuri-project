Kuri Site Pipeline — Authoritative Data Flow

Complete End-to-End Lifecycle: GIS Upload → Cadastral Resolution → Desk QC → QA Gate → Field Verification → Telecalling → Supplier Delivery
Updated Nov 3, 2026 • Kuri Architecture Standard
1. Upload & Sequence Minting
Admin Portal • /admin

Inputs: GIS Excel (12 columns: lat, lon, Village, Taluk, detection_type, area_sqm, priority_tier, etc.).

🌿 TNGIS WFS GeoServer Auto-Resolution: Point-in-polygon ray-casting against cadastral_analysis:fmb_ulpin auto-populates Survey No, Sub-division No, and ULPIN, canonicalizing Revenue Village & Taluk.
  • ID Minting: Creates immutable standard 6-digit CBE-XXXXXX (legacy coordinates mapped to old_unique_id).
  • Derived Attributes: area_sqft, 1,500 sq ft eligibility check, 40m duplicate scan, Google Maps link.
  • Dedup Outcome: Unchanged rows ignored; changed details update site; new sites created.
Status on Arrival: QC Pending
↓
2. Desk QC & Imagery Assessment
Data QC Portal • /auditor/dashboard

Actions: QC Analyst inspects Google Maps, Street View, and high-resolution VHR satellite imagery (Cartosat/Maxar).

  • Plot Validation: Confirms total plot >= 1,500 sq ft; records observable construction features (rebar, foundations).
  • Contact Enrichment: Gathers builder/contractor contact from site signboards + public registries with source URL.
  • Nearby Case: If construction is visible near pin, creates child site with unique 6-digit ID linked via Parent Site ID.
Status on Exit: QC Done
↓
3. QA Review Gate
Data QC Portal • /auditor/dashboard

Independent Verification: Senior QA reviewer validates evidence and imagery dates prior to authorizing ground travel.

Review Decision:
  • Approved: Releases site for operations assignment. Approved
  • Needs Correction: Loops back to Desk QC for re-evaluation.
  • Disapproved: Marked Not Eligible (resting state; re-checked on next satellite pass).
↓ (Gated on Approved)
4. Assignment & Dispatch
Admin Portal • /admin

Operations Dispatch: Field verifiers assigned based on Priority Tier (Tier-1, Tier-2, Tier-3) and geographical clustering.

Target Coordinates: Verifiers are routed strictly to QC-confirmed coordinates, never raw detection points.

Status on Exit: Field Pending
↓
5. Field Visit & Ground Verification
Field Verification Portal • /app/

GPS & On-Site Evidence: Mobile app records captured GPS, accuracy radius (m), and deviation from QC coordinates.

Conditional Form Logic:
  • Construction Actively Happening == 'Yes': Type of Construction & Stage of Construction are Mandatory.
  • Construction Actively Happening == 'No': Type & Stage are Optional/Disabled; verifier selects structured Inactivity Reason (Vacant Land, Stalled, Demolished).
  • Role-Attributed Contacts: Distinct slots for Owner Phone vs Contractor Phone (strict 10-digit mobile check ^[6-9]\d{9}$).
  • TNGIS Cross-Check: Verifiers can verify Survey / Sub-division parcel stones on ground.
Status on Exit: Completed / Closed
↓
6. Quality Loops: Revisit & Reaudit
Cross-Portal Governance
🔄 Wrong Number Loopback: If a telecaller reports invalid/wrong phone, the record routes to Field Revisit for phone re-acquisition (site remains active, not dropped).
  • Field Revisit: Dispatched when gate was locked, access denied, or contact missing.
  • Desk Reaudit: Triggered when field verifier reports coordinates discrepancy. Re-evaluates satellite imagery.
↓
7. Telecalling & Commercial Qualification
Telecaller Portal • /telecaller/dashboard

Eligibility Filter: Only records with at least one verified 10-digit mobile number enter telecalling queue.

  • Timezone Standard: All call logs and attempts standardized to IST (Asia/Kolkata).
  • Attempt History: Max 3 structured call attempts across Morning (10-1), Afternoon (2-5), and Evening (5-8).
  • Dispositions: CONSTRUCTION_CONFIRMED, FOLLOW_UP, NOT_INTERESTED, WRONG_NUMBER.
↓
8. Package & Client Delivery
Admin Portal • /admin/export

Executive Client Delivery File (Excel): Exported for Schnell and material suppliers.

  • Comprehensive Lead Profile: 6-digit Site ID, Old Unique ID cross-reference, TNGIS Survey/Sub-div, verified address, construction stage, floor count, verified phone numbers with role designation, priority tier, and data freshness timestamp.
  • Privacy Safeguards: Suppresses unverified contacts and declined leads.
